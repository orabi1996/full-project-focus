## 2. Key Milestones & Capabilities Delivered

### 2.1 The Enterprise AI Intelligence Layer (Prompt 35)
- **MadarX AI Copilot (Screen M29):** Central bilingual natural language assistant (Arabic & English) grounded strictly in authoritative database records and governed metrics.
- **Enterprise RAG & Policy Retrieval:** Semantic search over corporate policies, handbooks, and labor law regulations with verifiable source citations (`Document - Version - Section`).
- **Governed Mutation Gates:** Multi-step `Preview -> Confirm -> Execute -> Verify` gate requiring explicit human confirmation before executing any write actions (tasks, notifications, workflows).
- **Proactive Insights Center:** Factual, non-fabricated monitoring of expiring certifications, probation evaluation deadlines, attendance SLA deviations, and succession vulnerabilities.
- **AI Observability & Cost Tracking:** Live telemetry for token volume, per-model costs, latency breakdowns, and safety denial counters.
- **Golden Evaluation Framework:** Deterministic benchmark suite validating prompt injection defense, SQL injection interception, and RBAC boundary isolation.

### 2.2 Multi-Country Statutory Compliance (Prompt 34)
- **Egypt Pack (`EG`):** NOSI Law 148/2019 (11% / 18.75%), Martyrs Fund, Emergency Fund, Income Tax Law 7/2024 progressive brackets, CBE WPS.
- **Saudi Arabia Pack (`SA`):** GOSI & SANED Royal Decree M/134 (9.75% / 11.75%, Expat 2%), SAMA/Mudad WPS, Article 84/85 EOS.
- **Qatar Pack (`QA`):** GRSIA Social Insurance Law 24/2022 (7% / 14%), QCB SIF WPS standard, Article 54 EOS.
- **Oman Pack (`OM`):** Social Protection Fund RD 52/2023 (8% / 14%), CBO WPS format, Article 61 EOS.
- **Bahrain Pack (`BH`):** SIO Law 24/1976 & Law 78/2006 (8% / 16%, Expat 1% / 3%), LMRA/CBB WPS, Article 116 EOS.

### 2.3 Comprehensive HR Domain Suites (Prompts 1–33)
- **Core Operations:** Employee Master Directory, Organizational Charts, Multi-Level Workflows, Leave Balances, Biometric Attendance, and Dynamic Shift Rostering.
- **Payroll & Finance:** Production Payroll Runs, WPS Export Engine, Loans & EOSB Calculations, and Expense Reimbursements.
- **Talent Acquisition & Development:** Recruitment ATS, 360 Performance Appraisals, Workforce Planning, Onboarding & Probation, Internal Mobility, Learning Management & Skill Matrices, Succession Planning, and Employee Engagement Surveys.
- **Governance & Legal:** Employee Relations & Disciplinary Cases, Compensation & Total Rewards, Asset Management, Enterprise Reporting Studio, and Tamper-Evident Audit Trails.
